Theological Foundation

God Speaks Through Prophets & Prophetesses

God desires us to overcome life's challenges, enhance our character, and ultimately return to His presence in the hereafter. Yet, He doesn't anticipate us to navigate this journey on our own.

Holy Ghost Guidance

Instead, He provides guidance through the Holy Ghost, scriptures, and prophets. The Spirit illuminates truth and directs paths.

Chosen Vessels

A prophetess is an individual chosen by God to provide direction to all of humanity. They are set apart for divine communication.

Biblical Pattern

Throughout history, from figures like Abraham and Moses to modern-day living prophets, God consistently employs the pattern of using prophets to lead His children.
