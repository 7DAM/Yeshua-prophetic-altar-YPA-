Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Discipleship Matrix.

Is this Bible study course really free?

Yes. The 7-week discipleship matrix, daily devotionals, and final certification are completely free. We also offer a free 15-minute orientation call to help you get started. We believe the Word of God should be accessible to all.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Absolutely. Upon completing all 7 weeks and the final spiritual assessment, you will receive a digital Certification of Discipleship from the Yeshua Prophetic Altar, which you can share on LinkedIn or use for ministry credentials.

How much time do I need to commit per week?

We recommend setting aside 30-45 minutes daily for your devotional reading, plus 1 hour for the weekly prophetic teaching module. The matrix is designed to fit into the schedule of a working professional or student.

Is this course available for Kenyans living abroad?

Yes. The course is 100% digital and accessible globally. Kenyans in the diaspora (UK, USA, Canada, Australia) are highly encouraged to enroll. The orientation calls are conducted via WhatsApp or Zoom.

Who teaches this discipleship matrix?

The curriculum is authored and overseen by Apostle Joshua and Prophetess Lillian, drawing from decades of apostolic ministry, prophetic insight, and biblical scholarship at the Yeshua Prophetic Altar in Nairobi.
