The Question Every Believer Must Answer

"Is this from God or me?"

It's 2:00 AM. You've just received a prophetic word—maybe at a conference, maybe through a YouTube video, maybe as a sudden impression during prayer. Your heart is racing. You feel a divine urgency. But a quiet voice whispers: "Are you sure?"

This is the most critical question in the prophetic life. Because an untested word is not just dangerous—it is potentially devastating.

The Bible does not tell us to reject prophecy. It tells us to test it.

"Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world." — 1 John 4:1 (KJV)

The Greek word for "try" is dokimazo (δοκιμάζω)—it means to assay, to test, to prove. It's the same word used for testing the purity of gold. God is not offended when you test a word. He commands it.

Key Insight: A genuine prophetic word will always survive testing. If a word collapses under biblical examination, it was never from God in the first place. Testing protects you without quenching the Spirit.

The Discernment Crisis in the Modern Church

We are living in a prophetic explosion. Social media has given every self-proclaimed prophet a global platform. Livestream "prophetic hits" generate millions of views. And many believers are consuming prophetic content like fast food—quick, emotional, untested.

The result is a discernment crisis of unprecedented scale:

  • Believers making major life decisions based on vague "words" that were never confirmed
  • Leaders acting on untested prophecy and causing organizational damage
  • Families fractured by "prophetic" words that contradicted Scripture
  • Financial ruin from "God told me to invest" moments that were never tested

How to hear God's voice clearly is not a mystery reserved for spiritual elites. It is a learnable skill grounded in Scripture, confirmed by the Spirit, and validated by community. And it begins with the 3-Voice Rule.

01

The Voice of the Spirit (Internal Witness)

"The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God." — Romans 8:16

The first voice is the most intimate: the internal witness of the Holy Spirit. This is not an audible voice (though it can be). It is the deep, settled conviction in your spirit when God is speaking.

How to Recognize the Internal Witness:

Peace as an Umpire

Colossians 3:15 says, "Let the peace of God rule in your hearts." The Greek word for "rule" is brabeuo—meaning to act as an umpire. When God speaks, the peace of God makes the final call.

The "Amen" Response

A genuine word from God produces an "amen" in your spirit—a deep, resonant agreement that transcends emotion.

The "Check" or "Witness"

When a word is NOT from God, you'll feel a subtle unease—a "check" in your spirit. This is the Holy Spirit's warning system. Never ignore it.

Discernment Exercise: Before acting on any word, sit in silence for 15 minutes. Ask the Holy Spirit: "Is this from You?" Notice whether your spirit settles into peace or rises with anxiety. The answer is often immediate—but we're too busy to hear it.
02

The Voice of the Word (Scriptural Filter)

"To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them." — Isaiah 8:20

The second voice is the most objective: Scripture. The Bereans in Acts 17:11 were praised because they "searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so." Even the Apostle Paul's teaching was tested against the Word.

The Scriptural Filter Test:

Every prophetic word must pass through these 4 scriptural gates:

Gate 1
Does it align with God's character?

God is not the author of confusion (1 Cor 14:33). If the word creates chaos, pressure, or fear, it fails Gate 1.

Gate 2
Does it align with Scripture?

God will never contradict His own Word. If the word asks you to violate biblical principles, it fails Gate 2.

Gate 3
Does it point to Jesus?

Revelation 19:10 says "the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." If the word elevates the prophet over Christ, it fails Gate 3.

Gate 4
Does it produce good fruit?

Matthew 7:16 — "By their fruits ye shall know them." If the word produces manipulation, control, or division, it fails Gate 4.

03

The Voice of Counsel (External Confirmation)

"Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety." — Proverbs 11:14

The third voice is the most overlooked: mature spiritual counsel. In a culture of individualistic faith, we've forgotten that God speaks through community. The New Testament pattern is clear: major prophetic direction was always confirmed by others (Acts 13:1-3, Acts 15:28).

The 3-Voice Counsel Protocol:

Before acting on a major prophetic word, present it to these three specific voices:

Voice 1: Your Spiritual Covering

A pastor, apostle, or spiritual father/mother who knows your life and has authority over your growth. They will test the word against your season and assignment.

Voice 2: A Peer Who Is Ahead

Someone walking in the same calling but further along. They've faced similar decisions and can spot blind spots you can't see.

Voice 3: A Truth-Teller

Someone who will tell you the truth even if it's uncomfortable. Not a yes-person. Not an enabler. Someone who loves you enough to say "wait" or "no."

Critical Rule: If even ONE of your three voices raises a red flag, do not proceed. Pause. Investigate. Pray. A genuine word from God will gain confirmation, not lose it, when tested.

7 Red Flags: When to Reject a Prophetic Word

Even with the 3-Voice Rule, some words should be rejected immediately. Watch for these red flags:

1
Contradicts Scripture

If the word asks you to violate biblical principles, it is not from God. Period.

2
Creates Fear or Manipulation

"If you don't follow this word, God will punish you" is manipulation, not prophecy.

3
Elevates the Prophet

If the word makes you more dependent on the prophet than on Christ, it's a control mechanism.

4
Demands Immediate Action

God gives time to test. Pressure to act immediately without confirmation is a red flag.

5
No Peace in Your Spirit

If your spirit is unsettled, do not proceed. The peace of God is your umpire.

6
No Confirmation from Counsel

If no wise, mature person confirms the word, it's likely not from God.

7
Produces Division

Prophetic words build up the body. If the word causes division, it's not from the Spirit of unity.
